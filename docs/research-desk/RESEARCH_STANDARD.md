# Institutional Company Research Standard

A complete company pack is a dated, versioned, cited investment-committee deliverable—not a workflow status page.

Front-stage order:

1. Investment conclusion, thesis evolution, what changed and decision ask.
2. Five-to-ten-year growth, volume/price/mix and CAGR story.
3. Business, segment, geography, customer and product economics with reconciliation.
4. Full income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and capital allocation by year.
5. Profitability, return, cash-conversion, leverage, liquidity, reinvestment, turnover and per-share ratio library.
6. ROCE/ROIC decomposition with exact invested-capital inputs and reproducible formulas.
7. Management guidance versus delivery, governance and forensic review.
8. Industry, TAM, value chain, supply/demand, peers, moat and disconfirmers.
9. DCF, reverse DCF, multiples and scenario valuation only when supported, with sensitivities and expected return.
10. Catalysts, risks, kill conditions, timeline and committee decision.

Facts, management guidance, external estimates, scenarios and missing inputs must remain visually and semantically distinct. Evidence, parser state, agent operations and raw ledgers belong in per-section citation drawers or appendices.
